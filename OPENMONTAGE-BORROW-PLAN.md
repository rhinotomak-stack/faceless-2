## SFX — professional sound-designer rework (SHIPPED 2026-07-07, user's active pain point)
- [x] Root cause: SFX fired on **every non-cut transition** (incl. gentle crossfade/dissolve) + **every mapped MG** (every lowerThird/callout/focusWord dinged) → ~94 SFX in a 20-min video (1 every 13s) = amateur noise. Fix in `build-video.js` planSfxClips block: only **MOTIVATED transitions** (`MOTIVATED_TRANSITIONS` set: whip/zoom-punch/dip-black/push/flash/glitch/filmburn… via `normTx`) + only **IMPACT MG reveals** (`IMPACT_MG_TYPES`: statCounter/statCard/headline/keyTakeaway/timeline…) earn a sound; gentle transitions + ambient overlays are SILENT. `resolveTxSfx` maps modern kebab types (whip-right→sfx-whip) to the camelCase `TRANSITION_TO_SFX`. **Priority-first density budget** (≥`SFX_MIN_GAP_SEC`=2.5s): impact reveals (vol `SFX_VOL_IMPACT`=0.42) protect their slot over whooshes (vol `SFX_VOL_WHOOSH`=0.3); whoosh lead 0.15→0.04s. `SFX_LEGACY=1` restores old dense mode. Verified on real 20-min plan: **94→32 SFX** (17 impact + 15 whoosh, 2 crowding whooshes dropped), 1 every ~37s.
